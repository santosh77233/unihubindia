1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This Refund Policy explains the terms and conditions under which UniHub India Private Limited ("UniHub," "we," "us," or "our") provides refunds for payments made on our Platform.

1.2 Scope

This Policy applies to:

  • Application fees paid by Students
  • Subscription fees paid by Colleges
  • Any other fees charged by UniHub

This Policy DOES NOT cover:

  • Admission fees paid directly to colleges (governed by college policies)
  • Fees charged by third parties (payment gateway charges, bank fees)

1.3 Acceptance

By making a payment on the UniHub Platform, you accept the terms of this Refund Policy.

2. Application Fee Refunds (For Students)

2.1 Fee Structure Recap

  • Base application fee: ₹299 (covers up to 5 college applications)
  • Additional colleges: ₹49 per college
  • Fees are inclusive of GST

2.2 Money-Back Guarantee (100% Refund)

✅You are eligible for a FULL REFUND of application fees if:

Condition: ALL colleges you applied to reject your application.

How It Works:

  1. You apply to 5 colleges (paid ₹299)
  2. All 5 colleges reject your application
  3. You receive a full refund of ₹299 automatically

Refund Processing:

  • Automatic: No request needed
  • Timeline: Within 7 business days of final rejection
  • Method: Refunded to original payment method (UPI, card, bank account)

Important Notes:

  • Refund applies ONLY if every single application is rejected
  • If even ONE college accepts or shortlists you, NO refund is provided
  • "Rejection" means college explicitly rejected; applications stuck in "Under Review" for >30 days count as "pending," not "rejected"

2.3 Early Withdrawal Refund (Partial Refund)

If you withdraw your application(s) before any college reviews them, you may receive a partial refund.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Withdrawal request made within 24 hours of application submission
  • No college has opened or reviewed your application
  • Application status is "Submitted" (not "Under Review" or beyond)

Refund Amount:

  • 80% of application fee (₹239 for base fee of ₹299)
  • 20% processing fee retained by UniHub (covers payment gateway charges, platform costs)

How to Request:

  1. Log in to your UniHub account
  2. Go to Dashboard > My Applications
  3. Select the application(s) to withdraw
  4. Click "Withdraw & Request Refund"
  5. Confirm withdrawal

Processing Timeline:

  • Request reviewed within 2 business days
  • Refund processed within 7-10 business days if approved
  • Notification sent via email

2.4 Technical Error Refund (100% Refund)

If a technical error on our Platform prevents your application from being submitted, you are eligible for a full refund.

Examples of Technical Errors:

  • Payment successful but application not submitted
  • System crash during application submission
  • Document upload failure despite successful payment

How to Request:

  1. Contact support immediately at support@unihub.in
  2. Include: Application ID, Payment transaction ID, Screenshot of error, Description of issue

Processing:

  • Investigation within 48 hours
  • If technical error confirmed: 100% refund within 5 business days
  • If user error: No refund, guidance provided

2.5 Duplicate Payment Refund (100% Refund)

If you are charged twice for the same application due to a system error, the duplicate charge will be fully refunded.

Automatic Detection:

  • Our system automatically detects duplicate transactions
  • Refund initiated automatically within 3 business days

2.6 Non-Refundable Scenarios

Application fees are NOT refundable in the following cases:

❌ Change of Mind

  • You decide not to pursue admissions
  • You no longer want to apply to certain colleges
  • You found a better alternative

❌ Partial Acceptance/Rejection

  • At least one college accepted/shortlisted you
  • Mixed results (some accepted, some rejected)

❌ Application After Review Started

  • College has opened and reviewed your application
  • Application status is "Under Review," "Shortlisted," or beyond

❌ Offer Letter Received (Even if Declined)

  • You received an offer letter from any college
  • You declined the offer (this still counts as "successful application facilitation")

❌ Violation of Terms

  • Account suspended for policy violations
  • Fraudulent activity detected
  • Fake documents submitted

3. Admission Fee Refunds (Paid to Colleges)

3.1 UniHub's Role

Important: Admission fees are paid directly to colleges, not to UniHub. UniHub only facilitates the connection; we do not control college refund policies.

3.2 College Refund Policies

Each college has its own refund policy for admission fees. You must:

  • Review the college's refund policy before paying admission fees
  • Contact the college directly for admission fee refunds
  • Refer to your offer letter for refund terms

3.3 UniHub Assistance

If you face issues with college refunds:

  • We can mediate and contact the college on your behalf
  • We can escalate disputes through our dispute resolution process
  • We cannot force colleges to issue refunds beyond their stated policies

Contact: disputes@unihub.in

4. Subscription Fee Refunds (For Colleges)

4.1 Subscription Plans Recap

  • Basic Plan: ₹5,000/month
  • Standard Plan: ₹10,000/month
  • Premium Plan: ₹20,000/month
  • Enterprise Plan: Custom pricing

4.2 Refund Policy for Subscriptions

General Rule: Subscription fees are non-refundable once the billing cycle begins.

Exceptions (Pro-Rated Refund):

a) Service Downtime (>72 hours):

  • If UniHub Platform is down for more than 72 consecutive hours due to our fault
  • Refund calculation: (Days of downtime / 30) × Monthly subscription fee
  • Example: 5 days downtime on ₹10,000 plan = (5/30) × 10,000 = ₹1,667 refund

b) Account Termination by UniHub (Wrongful):

  • If we terminate your account incorrectly (and later reinstate it)
  • Full refund of unused subscription period

c) Downgrade Within 7 Days:

  • If you downgrade your plan within 7 days of upgrading
  • Refund of the difference between plans (pro-rated)

4.3 Cancellation Policy

Mid-Cycle Cancellation:

  • You can cancel your subscription anytime
  • No refund for the current billing cycle
  • Access continues until the end of the paid period
  • Subscription will not auto-renew

6. Refund Process

6.1 How Refunds Are Processed

Automatic Refunds:

  • Money-Back Guarantee (all applications rejected)
  • Duplicate payment detection
  • Service downtime compensation

No action needed – refund processed automatically.

Manual Refund Requests:

  • Early withdrawal
  • Technical error refunds
  • Subscription downgrades

Action needed – follow request process below.

6.2 Refund Request Process

Step 1: Submit Request

Email: refunds@unihub.in

Include:

  • Your full name and registered email
  • Application ID / Subscription ID / Transaction ID
  • Reason for refund request
  • Supporting documents (if applicable)

Step 2: Review

  • Our team reviews your request within 3 business days
  • We may request additional information

Step 3: Decision

  • You receive an email with refund decision (approved/rejected)
  • If rejected, detailed reason is provided

Step 4: Processing

  • If approved, refund is processed within 5-7 business days
  • Refund is sent to original payment method

Step 5: Confirmation

  • You receive refund confirmation email
  • Check your bank/card statement for refund credit (may take additional 3-5 days)

6.3 Refund Timeline Summary

Refund Type Review Time Processing Time Total Time
Money-Back Guarantee (Auto) N/A 7 days 7 days
Early Withdrawal (Manual) 2 days 7-10 days 9-12 days
Technical Error (Manual) 2 days 5 days 7 days
Duplicate Payment (Auto) N/A 3 days 3 days
Subscription Downgrade (Manual) 2 days 7 days 9 days

Note: Bank processing may add 3-5 days to the above timelines.

6.4 Refund Methods

Refunds are processed using the same method as the original payment:

  • UPI: Refunded to linked bank account
  • Credit/Debit Card: Refunded to same card
  • Net Banking: Refunded to source bank account
  • Wallet (Paytm, etc.): Refunded to wallet

Exception: If the original payment method is no longer active (card expired, account closed), we will request alternate bank details via email.

7. Failed Payments (Not Refunds)

7.1 Payment Failed vs. Refund

Payment Failed:

  • Your payment attempt was not successful
  • No money was deducted
  • You can retry payment immediately

Refund:

  • Payment was successful (money deducted)
  • You are requesting the money back

7.2 What Happens When Payment Fails

If your payment fails:

  • You receive an error message
  • Application is not submitted (remains in draft)
  • No refund needed (you were not charged)

7.3 Money Deducted But Payment Failed

In rare cases, money may be deducted but the payment is marked as "failed."

What Happens:

  • This is usually a temporary authorization hold by your bank
  • Money will be automatically refunded by your bank within 5-7 days
  • UniHub does not receive this money
  • You can retry payment immediately (you won't be charged twice)

If Not Refunded After 7 Days:

  • Contact your bank first (they hold the funds, not us)
  • Email us at payments@unihub.in with transaction details

8. Chargebacks and Disputes

8.1 Chargeback Definition

A chargeback is when you dispute a transaction with your bank/card issuer, and they reverse the payment.

8.2 Before Filing a Chargeback

Please contact us first at refunds@unihub.in before initiating a chargeback with your bank.

Why?

  • We can often resolve issues faster than the bank
  • Chargebacks incur penalties on us (unfair if the issue is resolvable)
  • Your account may be suspended during chargeback investigation

8.3 Chargeback Process

If you file a chargeback:

  1. Your bank notifies us of the dispute
  2. We investigate the transaction
  3. We respond to the bank with evidence
  4. Bank makes a decision (in your favor or ours)

8.4 Chargeback Consequences

If a chargeback is filed:

  • Your UniHub account may be temporarily suspended
  • If chargeback is found to be fraudulent, your account will be permanently banned
  • We reserve the right to pursue legal action for fraudulent chargebacks

11. Consumer Rights (India)

11.1 Consumer Protection Act, 2019

You have rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. If you believe your refund request was unfairly denied, you may:

a) File a Consumer Complaint:

  • District Consumer Forum: For claims up to ₹1 crore
  • State Consumer Commission: For claims ₹1 crore to ₹10 crore
  • National Consumer Commission: For claims above ₹10 crore

b) Online Complaint:

  • National Consumer Helpline: 1800-11-4000
  • Online portal: https://consumerhelpline.gov.in

11.2 UniHub's Commitment

We are committed to:

  • Resolving disputes fairly and in good faith

12. Quick FAQs

Q1: Will I get a refund if I change my mind?
A: No. Application fees are non-refundable if you simply change your mind.

Q2: What if all colleges reject me?
A: You get a 100% refund automatically within 7 business days (Money-Back Guarantee).

Q3: Can I get a refund if I withdraw within 24 hours?
A: Yes, 80% refund (if no college has reviewed your application yet).

Q4: What if one college accepts and others reject?
A: No refund. You received at least one admission opportunity.

Q5: How long do refunds take?
A: Typically 5-10 business days depending on refund type. Bank processing may add 3-5 days.

Q6: Can I get a refund if payment failed?
A: If payment failed, you were not charged. No refund needed. Just retry payment.

Q7: Are subscription fees refundable?
A: Generally no, except for service downtime >72 hours or plan downgrades within 7 days.

Q8: What if I was charged twice?
A: Duplicate charges are automatically refunded within 3 business days.

Q9: Can I get a refund for admission fees paid to colleges?
A: Admission fees are paid to colleges, not UniHub. Refunds are governed by college policies. Contact the college directly.

Q10: What if my refund request is denied?
A: You will receive a detailed reason. You can appeal by contacting our Grievance Officer or filing a consumer complaint.
