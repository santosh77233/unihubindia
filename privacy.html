1. Introduction

1.1 Our Commitment

UniHub India Private Limited ("UniHub," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our Platform (website, mobile app, and services).

1.2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • All users of the UniHub Platform (Students, Colleges, and other users)
  • Information collected through our website, mobile app, emails, and other interactions

1.3 Consent

By using the UniHub Platform, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Platform.

1.4 Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 and IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011
  • Applicable data protection and privacy laws in India

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

For Students:

a) Account Information:

  • Full name, date of birth, gender
  • Email address, phone number
  • Password (encrypted)

b) Academic Information:

  • 10th and 12th standard marks, board, school name
  • Entrance exam scores (JEE, NEET, CUET, state exams)
  • Academic documents (mark sheets, certificates)

c) Application Information:

  • Course preferences
  • Category (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, PWD)
  • Domicile certificate, caste certificate (if applicable)
  • Personal statement, essays (if required)

d) Identity Verification:

  • Aadhar number (masked, for verification only)
  • PAN card (for scholarship verification, if applicable)
  • Photograph

e) Payment Information:

  • Credit/debit card details (processed by payment gateway, not stored by us)
  • UPI ID, bank account details (for refunds)
  • Transaction history

f) Communication Data:

  • Messages sent through our Platform
  • Support tickets and correspondence
  • Feedback and reviews

For Colleges:

a) Institutional Information:

  • College name, type, affiliation
  • AICTE/UGC approval numbers, NAAC accreditation
  • Contact details (official email, phone, address)

b) Authorized User Information:

  • Names, email addresses, and roles of staff members accessing the Platform
  • Login credentials (encrypted)

c) Course Information:

  • Course names, fees, eligibility criteria
  • Seat matrix, cut-off data

d) Payment & Billing Information:

  • PAN, GST number
  • Bank account details (for payments)
  • Subscription and transaction history

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

a) Device & Browser Information:

  • IP address, device type, operating system
  • Browser type and version
  • Screen resolution, language preferences

b) Usage Data:

  • Pages visited, features used
  • Time spent on pages
  • Click patterns and navigation paths
  • Search queries

c) Location Data:

  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • Precise location (if you grant permission via mobile app)

d) Cookies and Similar Technologies:

We use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze usage patterns
  • Improve user experience
  • Deliver personalized content

Cookie Types:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for Platform functionality (cannot be disabled)
  • Performance Cookies: Collect anonymous usage data
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences
  • Advertising Cookies: Deliver relevant ads (with your consent)

Cookie Management: You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may impair Platform functionality.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

a) Social Media Login:

If you sign up using Google or other social media accounts, we receive:

  • Name, email, profile picture
  • As permitted by your social media privacy settings

b) Entrance Exam Authorities:

With your consent, we may verify:

  • JEE, NEET scores through official APIs
  • State exam results through government databases

c) Educational Boards:

We may verify academic records through:

  • CBSE, state board databases (with your authorization)

d) Payment Gateways:

We receive transaction status and payment confirmation from our payment partners (Razorpay, etc.)

e) Analytics Providers:

We use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, and similar tools to understand user behavior.

4. How We Share Your Information

4.1 With Colleges (For Students)

When you apply to a college through UniHub, we share your application data with that college, including:

  • Personal and academic information
  • Uploaded documents
  • Course preferences
  • Contact details

Purpose: To enable colleges to review your application and make admission decisions.

Your Control: You control which colleges receive your data by choosing which colleges to apply to.

4.2 With Students (For Colleges)

We display college information to students, including:

  • Institutional details
  • Course offerings and fees
  • Accreditation and rankings
  • Placement statistics

4.3 With Service Providers

We share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our Platform:

  • Payment Processors: Razorpay, PayU (for payment processing)
  • Cloud Hosting: AWS, Google Cloud (for data storage)
  • Email Services: SendGrid, Amazon SES (for transactional emails)
  • SMS Providers: Twilio, MSG91 (for SMS notifications)
  • Analytics: Google Analytics, Mixpanel (for usage analysis)
  • Customer Support: Zendesk, Freshdesk (for support tickets)

Terms: These providers are contractually obligated to:

  • Use your data only for specified purposes
  • Implement appropriate security measures
  • Not disclose your data to third parties

4.4 With Government Authorities

We may disclose your information to:

  • Law enforcement agencies (if legally required)
  • Regulatory bodies (AICTE, UGC) for compliance audits
  • Courts (in response to subpoenas or court orders)

4.5 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you via email and/or prominent notice on our Platform.

4.6 With Your Consent

We may share your information with third parties for purposes not listed above only with your explicit consent.

4.7 Aggregated and Anonymized Data

We may share aggregated, anonymized data that cannot identify you personally, such as:

  • Platform usage statistics
  • Industry trends and insights
  • Academic research (with anonymized datasets)

5. Data Security

5.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

a) Technical Safeguards:

  • Encryption: Data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Secure Servers: Hosted on ISO 27001 certified cloud infrastructure
  • Firewalls: Network-level and application-level firewalls
  • Access Controls: Role-based access with multi-factor authentication
  • Regular Audits: Penetration testing and vulnerability assessments

b) Organizational Safeguards:

  • Data Minimization: We collect only necessary data
  • Employee Training: Staff trained on data protection practices
  • Confidentiality Agreements: All employees sign NDAs
  • Incident Response Plan: Procedures for data breach response

5.2 Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach:

  • We will notify affected users within 72 hours of discovery
  • Notification will include: nature of breach, data affected, remedial measures
  • We will report to relevant authorities as required by law

5.3 Your Responsibility

You are responsible for:

  • Keeping your password secure
  • Not sharing login credentials
  • Logging out from shared devices
  • Using secure internet connections

We are NOT liable for unauthorized access resulting from your failure to secure your account.

6. Data Retention

6.1 Retention Periods

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide services to you
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

Specific Retention Periods:

For Students:

  • Active accounts: Data retained while account is active
  • Application data: 7 years from application submission (for audit/compliance)
  • Payment records: 7 years (as per Indian tax laws)
  • Deleted accounts: Data deleted within 90 days, except as required by law

For Colleges:

  • Active subscriptions: Data retained during subscription period
  • Application data: 7 years (for compliance and records)
  • Cancelled subscriptions: Data retained for 1 year, then deleted

6.2 Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your data by contacting privacy@unihub.in. We will process your request within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Note: Some data may be retained in anonymized form for analytics.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Under Indian data protection laws, you have the following rights:

7.1 Right to Access

You can access your personal information through your account dashboard or by requesting a copy from privacy@unihub.in.

7.2 Right to Correction

You can update inaccurate information through your account settings or by contacting us.

7.3 Right to Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and data, subject to legal retention requirements.

7.4 Right to Data Portability

You can request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format (CSV, JSON).

7.5 Right to Withdraw Consent

You can withdraw consent for:

  • Marketing communications (unsubscribe links in emails)
  • Location tracking (mobile app settings)
  • Cookie tracking (browser settings)

7.6 Right to Object

You can object to processing of your data for:

  • Direct marketing (opt-out anytime)
  • Automated decision-making (request human review)

7.7 Right to Complain

If you believe we have violated your privacy rights, you can:

  • Contact our Grievance Officer (see Section 12)
  • File a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India (once established under DPDPA 2023)

8. Children's Privacy

8.1 Age Restriction

Our Platform is intended for users aged 16 years and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 without parental consent.

8.2 Parental Consent

If a user is between 16-18 years old:

  • Parental/guardian consent is required
  • Parents can access and manage the minor's account
  • We may request proof of parental consent

8.3 Minors Under 16

If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 16 without parental consent, we will delete such data within 30 days.

Parents: If you believe your child has provided information without consent, contact us at privacy@unihub.in.

Your data is primarily stored on servers located in India. We use cloud service providers (AWS, Google Cloud) with data centers in India.

9.2 Transfers Outside India

In some cases, your data may be transferred outside India to:

  • Cloud service providers with international data centers (e.g., AWS backups)

9.3 Safeguards for International Transfers

When data is transferred outside India, we ensure:

  • Service providers comply with Indian data protection standards
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent protections are in place
  • Data is encrypted during transfer
