Overview

Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (IIT Kharagpur or IIT KGP) is a public technical and research university established by the Government of India in 1951. It is the first of the IITs to be established and is recognized as an Institute of Eminence by the Government of India.

The institute is located in Kharagpur in West Bengal and has a campus of about 8.5 square kilometers (3.3 sq mi). The campus is residential in nature, with more than 22,000 residents, including students, faculty, and staff.

Why Choose IIT Kharagpur?

  • First IIT established in India, with over 70 years of academic excellence
  • Consistently ranked among top 5 engineering institutes in India
  • Strong industry connections with Fortune 500 companies
  • State-of-the-art research facilities and laboratories
  • Vibrant campus life with 50+ clubs and societies
  • Alumni network of over 60,000+ across the globe

Key Highlights

  • Faculty: 600+ distinguished faculty members
  • Research Output: 1,500+ research papers published annually
  • International Collaborations: 200+ partner universities worldwide
  • Entrepreneurship: 400+ startups incubated

Eligibility & Cutoffs

Eligibility Criteria (B.Tech)

  • Educational Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics
  • Minimum Marks: 75% aggregate (65% for SC/ST/PwD candidates)
  • Entrance Exam: JEE Advanced (Must qualify JEE Main)
  • Age Limit: Maximum 25 years (30 for SC/ST/PwD)
  • Number of Attempts: Maximum 2 consecutive attempts
